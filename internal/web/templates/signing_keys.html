Signing keys

The JWKS and SAML metadata publish these keys. Rotating signs new tokens and assertions with a new key and keeps the old key published for the grace period, so you can test how your app refetches keys and replaces a pinned certificate. An expired, not-yet-valid, or no-expiration certificate tests whether your app checks certificate dates; the new key still signs correctly.
